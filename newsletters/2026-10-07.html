Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six more terms your portfolio managers and analysts use daily — vocabulary that helps you hold your own when the conversation turns to valuation, execution, and fund mechanics.

NAV (Net Asset Value)
Total fund assets minus liabilities, divided by shares outstanding. The benchmark price for mutual funds and ETFs. AI platforms for fund analysis must compute and track NAV changes in near real-time.
P/E Ratio
Price-to-Earnings: share price divided by earnings per share. A high P/E signals growth expectations; a low one, value. AI valuation tools use P/E alongside other multiples to flag over- and under-priced securities.
Beta
Measures an asset's volatility relative to the market. Beta of 1.0 = moves with the market; >1.0 = amplified swings; <1.0 = dampened. Crucial for risk-adjusted portfolio construction — AI tools model beta under stress scenarios.
AUM (Assets Under Management)
The total market value of investments a firm or fund manages. A $10B AUM fund and a $100M one face very different liquidity constraints — your product's design must account for the scale of the client it serves.
VWAP
Volume-Weighted Average Price — the average price of a security weighted by trading volume across the day. Traders use it as a benchmark for execution quality. AI execution tools optimise orders around the VWAP to minimise market impact.
Sector Rotation
Moving capital between industry sectors (e.g., from tech to energy) in response to economic cycle shifts. Portfolio managers use AI to detect rotation signals earlier — a core use case for alternative data and NLP tools.
