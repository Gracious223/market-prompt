Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Today's stories touch fixed income, credit structure, and market risk. These six terms will help you decode the conversations happening around you — and ask sharper questions in product discovery.

Securitization
Bundling illiquid assets (mortgages, loans) into tradable securities like ABS or RMBS. dv01's agentic platform targets exactly this workflow — collateral analysis for securitized products.
Collateral
An asset pledged to back a loan or trading position. In securitization, the underlying loans are the collateral. In repo markets, gilts or bonds serve this role. Quality matters — not all collateral is equal.
Credit Spread
The yield difference between a corporate bond and a risk-free government bond of the same maturity. Spreads widen when markets price in more default risk — an early-warning signal for analysts.
Duration
A bond's sensitivity to interest rate changes. A bond with duration of 5 loses ~5% in value for each 1% rise in rates. Portfolio managers use duration to manage interest rate exposure.
Concentration Risk
Overexposure to a single asset, sector, or counterparty. The BoE's AI debt warning is essentially a concentration risk flag — too much capital tied to one theme. Regulators and analysts watch for this.
Fiduciary Duty
The legal obligation to act in a client's best interest. In wealth management, every AI-assisted recommendation carries fiduciary weight. This is why auditability isn't optional — it's liability management.
