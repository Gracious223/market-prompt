Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six more terms your analysts and portfolio managers use daily — and what they mean for the AI products you build for them.

Price-to-Earnings (P/E)
A stock's price divided by its earnings per share. The most common valuation metric in equity analysis. AI tools that track P/E across a portfolio and flag outliers relative to sector peers are among the fastest-shipped use cases in equity research.
Free Cash Flow (FCF)
Cash generated by a business after capital expenditure. Often considered a more reliable profitability signal than earnings, because it's harder to manipulate. Analysts building AI-assisted DCF models start here.
Duration
A bond's sensitivity to interest rate changes — measured in years. A portfolio with long duration loses more value when rates rise. AI tools that track portfolio duration in real time are critical for fixed income PMs navigating central bank decisions.
Drawdown
The peak-to-trough decline in a portfolio's value over a given period. Risk managers and PMs monitor maximum drawdown closely. AI tools that simulate drawdown under stress scenarios are high-value for risk-adjusted portfolio construction.
EBITDA
Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortisation. Used to assess operational profitability, independent of capital structure. Widely used in credit analysis and private equity — your users will reference it in any fundamental research conversation.
Sharpe Ratio
Return per unit of risk taken — calculated as excess return divided by volatility. A Sharpe above 1 is considered good. AI tools that compare portfolio Sharpe ratios across time periods and benchmark strategies are standard in quant PM workflows.

SEC Brings Six AI Fraud Cases Totalling $44M+ — All Under Existing Law

The SEC is not writing new AI rules — it is enforcing through existing antifraud, disclosure, and fiduciary frameworks. Primary focus: "AI washing" — false or overstated capability claims in marketing materials or client disclosures. Six named cases since March 2024 carry over $44M in alleged fraud, with several million in penalties already assessed.

Why It Matters

AI washing is now a legal risk, not just a credibility problem. The framing distinction between "our AI predicts alpha" and "our AI surfaces signals for analyst review" has regulatory consequences. This should shape every word of your product descriptions and disclosures.
