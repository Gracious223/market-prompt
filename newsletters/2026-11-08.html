Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six more concepts your portfolio managers and analysts use daily — and that you need to speak fluently when scoping tools, writing PRDs, or sitting in investment team meetings.

Short Selling
Borrowing shares to sell them now, expecting to buy them back cheaper later and profit from the decline. AI-powered short-side research is a growing use case.
Market Capitalisation
Share price × shares outstanding. Large-cap (>$10B), mid-cap ($2–10B), small-cap (<$2B). Determines which universe an analyst covers.
Yield Curve
A chart of bond yields by maturity. When short-term yields exceed long-term ones (inverted curve), it typically signals recession risk — closely watched by macro analysts.
Sharpe Ratio
Return above the risk-free rate divided by volatility. Higher is better. A key metric for comparing AI-generated strategy performance on a risk-adjusted basis.
Drawdown
The peak-to-trough decline in portfolio value over a period. Maximum drawdown is a critical risk metric — your users care about how deep losses can go, not just average returns.
Fundamental Analysis
Evaluating a security by studying financials, management, competitive position, and macro conditions. The core workflow your AI tools are automating — or attempting to.

Bank of England: Agentic AI Is a Systemic Risk — Bespoke Rules on the Way

The BoE's July 2026 Financial Stability Report named frontier AI a systemic risk on two fronts: AI-amplified cyberattacks and concentrated third-party dependencies. Governor Bailey called AI-enabled cyber risk "the most immediate concern" for global finance in a letter to G20 ministers. Why It Matters
