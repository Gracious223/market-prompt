Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Earnings season is here — and with hyperscaler capex in focus, this week demands fluency in market structure and valuation basics. Six terms every AI PM building for analysts and portfolio managers must own.

P/E Ratio
Price divided by earnings per share. The most common valuation multiple. A high P/E signals the market expects rapid growth — AI stocks trade at stretched P/Es, so your users track consensus vs. actual closely during earnings season.
Capex (Capital Expenditure)
Money spent on physical infrastructure — data centres, GPUs, network. When Alphabet or Microsoft raises 2027 capex guidance, it signals AI investment acceleration. When they cut it, the market often re-rates the whole sector downward.
Basis Points (bps)
One hundredth of a percentage point. 100bps = 1%. Used universally in rates, credit, and fees. If your product surfaces cost or yield data, you will need to output in basis points to be taken seriously by fixed income analysts.
Earnings Surprise
The gap between actual reported earnings and analyst consensus estimates. A positive surprise typically moves a stock up sharply. AI tools that extract and compare surprise magnitude across filings in real time are among the most actively used in buy-side research.
Sector Rotation
Systematic shifting of capital between sectors (e.g., from defensives into technology) based on macro conditions. Portfolio managers time rotations around earnings catalysts. An AI product that detects rotation signals early is extremely high value.
Rebalancing
Periodically adjusting portfolio weights back to target allocations — e.g., trimming a position that has grown too large. Agentic AI tools are increasingly being used to trigger and execute rebalancing automatically within risk limits.
