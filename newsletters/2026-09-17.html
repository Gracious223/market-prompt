Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

The vocabulary your portfolio managers and analysts use daily — mastering it lets you ask sharper questions and frame your product decisions in terms they recognise.

Yield Spread
The difference in yield between two bonds — typically a corporate bond vs. a government bond. Wider spreads signal higher perceived risk. AI tools that monitor spread changes in real time are growing in value.
Drawdown
The peak-to-trough decline in a portfolio's value over a given period. Managing maximum drawdown is a core constraint for portfolio managers. Risk-alert tools built on drawdown thresholds are a high-value AI application.
Net Asset Value (NAV)
Total assets minus liabilities of a fund, divided by shares outstanding. NAV is the price at which fund shares are bought and sold. Accuracy and timeliness of NAV calculation is a key operations challenge AI is being applied to.
Benchmark
A reference index (e.g., FTSE 100, S&P 500) used to measure a portfolio's performance. Almost all analyst tools are built around benchmark-relative thinking — understand which benchmarks your users track.
Systematic vs. Discretionary
Systematic investing uses rules-based, often algorithmic strategies. Discretionary uses human judgment. Most large funds blend both — AI is shifting the boundary between them, blurring where the model ends and the PM begins.
Tracking Error
How closely a portfolio's returns follow its benchmark. A low tracking error means it mirrors the index. A high one means active bets. AI products that help managers understand and control tracking error are well-positioned.

EU AI Act · High-Risk Deadline

Credit Scoring AI Is Now Formally High-Risk Under EU Law — Fines Up to €35M

From 2 August 2026, the EU AI Act's Annex III classifies credit-scoring AI as high-risk. Providers must complete conformity assessments, maintain full technical documentation, register in the EU database, and implement post-market monitoring. Deployers (not just builders) carry duties too.

Why It Matters

If your product touches any credit, lending, or risk-scoring workflow in an EU context, your PRD now has a mandatory compliance section. Explainability, auditability, and human oversight are not features — they're legal obligations.

Research & Think About
  • Read Openlayer's July 2026 EU AI Act compliance guide for credit scoring — the most current practical resource available
  • Understand what "conformity assessment" requires vs. what "post-market monitoring" requires — they are distinct obligations
  • Are any of your firm's products or workflows deployed in EU markets? If so, flag this deadline to your team
