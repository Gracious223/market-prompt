Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six terms your analysts use daily — especially relevant this week as markets react to AI capex pressures and the valuation debate over high-growth tech intensifies.

Basis Points (bps)
One hundredth of a percentage point. A 25 bps rate cut = 0.25%. Analysts use bps to discuss small but significant moves precisely — a spread widening 50 bps is a meaningful credit event that changes fund positioning.
P/E Ratio
Price divided by earnings per share — a rough measure of how expensive a stock is relative to its profits. High-P/E AI stocks are priced for high future growth; when that growth is questioned, they reprice sharply.
Short Selling
Borrowing shares to sell now, intending to buy back cheaper later. When sentiment turns negative on AI stocks, short sellers amplify downward moves. Heavy short interest is a signal analysts actively track.
Max Drawdown
The largest peak-to-trough decline of a portfolio or asset over a period. This week's AI stock rout is a drawdown event. PMs use it to measure downside risk and set stop-loss thresholds in mandates.
Earnings Season
The period each quarter when public companies report results — typically 6 weeks after quarter-end. AI infrastructure capex guidance in Q3 earnings is the signal your analysts are most focused on this October.
Liquidity Premium
The extra return investors demand for holding harder-to-sell assets. Rising borrowing costs this week widened the liquidity premium for riskier AI infrastructure names — a dynamic your PMs priced in real time.
