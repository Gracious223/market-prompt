The $12.4B Agentic Shift: CFOs Choose Agents Over Copilots

A ChatFin analysis of 2026 finance AI budgets finds the market has hit $12.4B — and 76% of CFOs are directing funds toward autonomous agents that act, not assistants that only suggest. Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six terms that come up constantly on institutional desks — and that you need to understand before you can design tools your users will trust.

Market Capitalisation
Share price × total shares outstanding. The single most-used measure of company size. "Large-cap", "mid-cap" and "small-cap" determine which index a stock belongs to — and which analysts cover it.
NAV (Net Asset Value)
The value of a fund's assets minus its liabilities, divided by shares outstanding. Critical for ETFs and mutual funds. A fund trading at a premium or discount to NAV is a signal analysts watch closely.
Duration
A bond's sensitivity to interest rate changes. Higher duration = more price movement per rate shift. When rates rise, long-duration bonds fall harder. AI tools modelling fixed income must handle duration correctly.
Sector Rotation
The movement of capital between industry sectors as economic conditions change. Analysts track rotation to identify where institutional money is flowing — and where it's leaving. A key signal your product might surface.
Market Maker
A firm that continuously quotes both buy and sell prices, providing liquidity to markets. Market makers profit from the bid-ask spread. In thin markets, the spread widens — a sign of stress that AI monitoring tools can flag.
Earnings Season
The quarterly period when most public companies report results — roughly Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct. One of the highest-volume periods for analyst activity. Your product's busiest window and most demanding test.

BCG's Executive Perspective on agentic AI warns that most finance teams are still at the copilot stage. Real transformation requires redesigning end-to-end workflows so agents execute while humans own judgment and exceptions — not bolting automation onto existing processes.

Governance

Moody's: Agentic AI in Financial Services Creates New Risk Taxonomy

Moody's CreditView analysis identifies that agentic finance workflows raise novel risks: each autonomous step concludes with a consequential action the institution must later substantiate. Regulators are only beginning to develop frameworks for oversight of agents that execute.

Why It Matters

Auditability is not a feature you add later. Every consequential action your agent takes must be traceable, explainable, and attributable to a named accountable person under SM&CR.

US Regulators

US Bank Regulators Narrow SR 11-7 — Agentic AI Excluded from Model Risk Rules

In May 2026 the Federal Reserve, OCC, and FDIC narrowed SR 11-7 model risk guidance to exclude generative and agentic AI. FSOC simultaneously stood up a standing AI Working Group. The practical effect: agentic systems in banks now operate in a governance vacuum until new guidance arrives.

Why It Matters

A governance vacuum is a double-edged sword: it removes a compliance obstacle but also removes the safety rails your product can point to. Firms building now are defining what good looks like.

Research & Think About
  • Read the CASRAI guide on how US financial regulators treat frontier AI
  • Understand SR 11-7 — even if it no longer covers your product, its framework is the mental model regulators use
  • Research what FSOC's AI Working Group has published so far

Agentic PM

The Agentic PM: From Chatbot Features to Orchestrating Multi-Step Agents

The Institute of Product Management's 2026 guide identifies a new PM archetype: the agentic PM, who designs and governs systems of autonomous agents rather than single-model features. MCP is becoming a standard for connecting agents to tools — and PMs who understand it are in demand.

Why It Matters

Designing a chatbot feature and designing an agentic system are not the same job. An agentic PM must define agent boundaries, failure modes, human escalation paths, and audit trails — skills that are rare and newly valued in financial services.

Research & Think About
  • Read about MCP (Model Context Protocol) — understand what it standardises and why it matters for multi-agent products
  • Design on paper: what would a three-agent system for your analysts look like? What does each agent own?
  • Research "vibe coding" — the ability to prototype agents without writing full code is a PM superpower right now
