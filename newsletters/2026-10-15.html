Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

This week's terms focus on how markets price risk and capital — the language your portfolio managers use daily when AI debt issuance, rate moves, and market breadth all shift at once.

Basis Points (bps)
One basis point = 0.01%. Used for yield and rate changes. "50bps" means 0.5%. Essential vocabulary when reading any market pulse or fixed income commentary.
Drawdown
The peak-to-trough decline in portfolio value. A 15% drawdown means the portfolio fell 15% from its highest point. AI tools increasingly model and limit maximum drawdown.
Sharpe Ratio
Return per unit of risk taken. Higher is better. A Sharpe of 1.0 means one unit of return for each unit of volatility. AI products help PMs optimise Sharpe across portfolios.
Short Selling
Borrowing and selling a security you don't own, then buying it back at a lower price to profit. Hedge funds short overvalued stocks. AI is increasingly used to identify short candidates.
Mark-to-Market
Valuing assets at current market price, not historical cost. Critical for daily P&L reporting. AI tools must account for mark-to-market when calculating real-time portfolio exposure.
Liquidity Premium
Extra return demanded for holding hard-to-sell assets. AI-related debt and private credit carry liquidity premiums over Treasuries. PMs factor this into expected return calculations.
