Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six terms your analysts use daily that you need in your vocabulary — especially as AI tools increasingly surface, score, and act on these concepts without explaining them.

Mark-to-Market (MTM)
Valuing a portfolio at current market prices, not what you paid. AI tools that surface real-time MTM moves give portfolio managers actionable signals, not just historical data.
NAV (Net Asset Value)
Total fund assets minus liabilities, divided by shares outstanding. For ETFs and mutual funds, NAV is the reference price. An AI-managed ETF's NAV is updated intraday — a design consideration for your dashboards.
Duration
A bond's sensitivity to interest rate changes, measured in years. A 10-year duration bond falls ~10% if rates rise 1%. AI fixed-income tools use duration to flag portfolio rate risk automatically.
Yield Spread
The difference in yield between two bonds — typically a corporate bond vs. a government bond. Widening spreads signal rising credit risk. AI models are being used to predict spread movements from earnings call sentiment.
Systematic vs. Discretionary
Systematic funds use rules and algorithms; discretionary funds rely on human judgment. AI products must be designed differently for each — systematic desks want raw signals, discretionary PMs want curated narratives.
Sharpe Ratio
Return earned per unit of risk. A higher Sharpe = better risk-adjusted performance. Analysts use it to compare portfolios fairly. If your product helps improve it, you have a clear ROI story.

FCA Publishes Landmark Good-and-Poor-Practice AI Guide for Financial Firms

The FCA published its "AI in Financial Services: Good Practice and Areas for Improvement" paper on October 19 — drawing on 2026 firm testing across three areas: consumer centricity, accountability and governance, and operational resilience. Poor practice flagged includes building AI tools without a defined need, excluding vulnerable consumers by default, and deploying automated journeys without adequate testing. The publication integrates six of seven Mills Review recommendations.

Why It Matters

This is not future risk — it's active oversight. Firms are already being tested. Overstating model accuracy, skipping pre-deployment testing, or lacking escalation triggers are findings the FCA is actively citing. Your PRD needs a governance section now.

Research & Think About

  • Draft the accountability question for your product: under SM&CR, who at your firm is personally responsible when your AI output causes harm?
