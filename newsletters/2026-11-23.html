If bond yields rise and AI agents across multiple firms simultaneously shift to defensive postures, each individual trade looks "suitable" — but the aggregate effect is a correlated sell-off no single firm triggered. Diversification assumes independence of decisions.

Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six more building blocks of the language your users speak every day — each one shapes decisions your product will ultimately inform.

Earnings Per Share (EPS)
A company's net profit divided by its number of outstanding shares. One of the most watched metrics in earnings season — analysts build models to forecast it quarters in advance.
Bid-Ask Spread
The gap between the highest price a buyer will pay (bid) and the lowest a seller will accept (ask). A wider spread signals lower liquidity; for large orders, it's a real transaction cost.
Book Value
Total assets minus total liabilities — what shareholders would theoretically receive if the firm were wound up today. Used to calculate price-to-book (P/B), a key valuation ratio.
Free Cash Flow (FCF)
Cash generated by a business after capital expenditure. Preferred by many analysts over reported earnings because it's harder to manipulate with accounting choices.
Liquidity
How quickly and cheaply an asset can be converted to cash without moving its price. AI tools surface liquidity signals in real time — but the concept is core to every portfolio decision.
Systematic vs. Discretionary
Systematic strategies follow rules or algorithms; discretionary managers make judgement calls. AI is blurring the boundary — and your product sits at the intersection of both.

UK Regulators Issue First Joint AI Cyber Guidance — Prompt Injection and Agent Attacks Named

The Bank of England, FCA, PRA, and HM Treasury published joint guidance on AI cyber risk on 19 November 2026. It explicitly names prompt injection, model and data poisoning, adversarial inputs, jailbreaking, autonomous AI agent attacks, and deepfakes as live threats to financial stability. The guidance is non-binding effective practices, not rules — but it signals the supervisory direction of travel.

Why It Matters

This is the first time UK regulators have explicitly named agentic AI threats in a joint publication. Your product's threat model now has regulatory language around it. Build your security review before your regulator asks for it.
