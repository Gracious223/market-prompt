Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Your analysts and portfolio managers navigate these terms daily. Understanding them precisely — not approximately — is what lets you build products they trust and use.

Short Selling
Borrowing shares to sell now, aiming to buy them back cheaper later. A profit from price declines. AI tools surface short interest data as a contrarian sentiment signal for analysts.
Market Capitalisation
Share price multiplied by outstanding shares. Large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap define risk and liquidity tiers. AI screening tools filter universes by market cap to match portfolio mandates.
Yield Curve
A chart of bond yields across maturities (e.g. 2yr vs. 10yr). An inverted curve — short rates above long — historically signals recession. Macro analysts use AI to monitor curve shape in real time.
Basis Points (bps)
One hundredth of one percent (0.01%). Rate moves, credit spreads, and fee differences are quoted in bps. AI models track portfolio duration and credit risk exposure in bps to match risk limits.
EPS (Earnings Per Share)
Net income divided by diluted shares outstanding — the headline metric of earnings season. AI tools predict EPS surprises using alternative data and NLP on management guidance.
Liquidity
How quickly an asset converts to cash without significant price impact. AI execution systems optimise trade timing to access deep liquidity and minimise market impact costs for large orders.
