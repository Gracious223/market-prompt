Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Your portfolio managers and analysts live inside a specific vocabulary every day. These six terms are the structural scaffolding for understanding how capital moves across markets — and how AI is beginning to reshape each one.

Market Capitalisation
Share price × total shares outstanding. "Large cap" (£10B+), "mid cap," "small cap" — the size buckets that drive index membership, liquidity, and analyst coverage. AI tools often filter the investable universe by market cap first.
Yield Curve
A plot of bond yields across different maturities. An "inverted" curve (short rates higher than long rates) has historically preceded recessions. AI models increasingly monitor yield curve dynamics as a macro signal.
Liquidity Premium
The extra return demanded for holding an illiquid asset — one that's hard to sell quickly without moving the price. Private equity and small-cap positions carry a liquidity premium. AI is redefining how firms estimate it.
Earnings Season
The ~4-week period each quarter when most public companies report results. It's the highest-intensity period for analysts and PMs. AI tools for earnings call summarisation and guidance extraction are now mainstream.
Sovereign Bonds
Debt issued by national governments — UK Gilts, US Treasuries, German Bunds. Considered near-risk-free in developed markets. They set the "risk-free rate" that underpins almost every valuation model analysts use.
Sector Rotation
Shifting capital between economic sectors (e.g. from technology to energy) as macro conditions change. PMs use sector rotation as a tactical lever. AI is being used to detect rotation signals earlier from alternative data.

FCA · UK · Breaking

FCA Publishes "Our Approach to Agentic AI" — Authorisation May Be Required at Launch

On November 24, the FCA published its landmark agentic AI guidance. The core principle: whether FCA regulation applies depends on what the agent does. An agent initiating payments or executing trades crosses the statutory perimeter. General information lookups stay outside it.

Why It Matters

This is the most important regulatory document for your product this year. If your agent touches execution or payment flows, it may require FCA authorisation before you launch. This is a go/no-go product decision, not a legal footnote.

Research & Think About

Accountability · SM&CR

FCA: Senior Managers Personally Accountable for Third-Party AI Agents Deployed in Their Area

The FCA's November 24 guidance makes clear that under SM&CR, senior managers are accountable for any AI model in their area — whether built in-house or by a third party. The agent's autonomy does not reduce the responsibility of the person who deployed it.

Why It Matters

Your stakeholders — the senior managers who approve your product — now have personal liability for its failures. This changes the conversation: explainability, auditability, and human override are not features; they are accountability mechanisms.

Research & Think About
  • Read the FCA/CMA/ICO/Ofcom joint statement on personal AI agents (October 30, 2026)
  • What is SM&CR? Learn the five conduct rules — they apply to anyone who could act on your AI's output
  • Design an "AI accountability map" for your product: who owns each decision the agent makes?
