Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

These six concepts come up constantly in conversations with portfolio managers and analysts. Build fluency here and you will follow their reasoning — and earn the room faster.

Yield Curve
A graph plotting interest rates on bonds of different maturities. An inverted yield curve (short-term rates higher than long-term) has historically preceded recessions — it is one of the most watched macro signals.
Duration
A bond's sensitivity to interest rate changes. A bond with 5-year duration falls ~5% if rates rise 1%. Duration is a core risk measure for fixed-income analysts and a key parameter AI tools must surface accurately.
Beta
How much a stock moves relative to the market. Beta of 1.5 means the stock moves 50% more than the index. High-beta stocks are riskier in downturns — this matters when portfolio managers talk about risk appetite.
Sharpe Ratio
Return earned per unit of risk taken (excess return ÷ standard deviation). The higher the Sharpe, the better the risk-adjusted performance. Portfolio managers use it to compare strategies fairly.
Liquidity Premium
The extra return investors demand for holding a less liquid asset. Illiquid assets (private credit, small-cap equities) must offer higher expected returns to compensate for the difficulty of selling quickly.
NAV (Net Asset Value)
Total assets minus liabilities, divided by shares outstanding — the per-share value of a fund. For ETFs and mutual funds, NAV is calculated daily and is the benchmark against which trading price is judged.

FCA Publishes Landmark AI Good-Practice Guidance — and Advances Six Mills Review Recommendations

The FCA's "AI in Financial Services: Good Practice and Areas for Improvement" paper — published 19 October — sets out what good AI governance looks like across the full AI lifecycle, from scoping to decommissioning. It draws on its AI Lab and Supercharged Sandbox. Alongside it, the FCA confirmed it is taking forward six of seven Mills Review recommendations on AI in retail financial services.

Why It Matters

This is non-binding guidance — but it is what supervisors will reference in examinations. SMF ownership of AI risks, audit trails, and consumer outcome accountability are now baseline expectations, not aspirational ones.

Research & Think About
  • Read the FCA's paper directly at fca.org.uk — the "good practice" examples are your product governance checklist
  • What does "clear SMF ownership for AI risks" mean for your team's accountability structure?
  • Under Consumer Duty: map every AI output your product produces to a "consumer outcome" your firm is accountable for
