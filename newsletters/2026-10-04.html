Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six terms your portfolio managers and analysts use daily — and that your product decisions should be grounded in.

Short Selling
Borrowing a security and selling it, hoping to buy it back cheaper later. Short sellers profit from price declines. AI is increasingly used to flag conditions that historically precede short-squeeze events.
Liquidity Premium
The extra return investors demand for holding assets that are harder to sell quickly. Less liquid assets command higher yields. AI tools that surface liquidity risk in portfolios are a high-priority use case.
Mark-to-Market (MTM)
Valuing a portfolio at current market prices rather than historical cost. MTM creates real-time unrealised P&L. AI-driven MTM dashboards help analysts monitor exposure continuously, not just at day-end.
Drawdown
The peak-to-trough decline in a portfolio's value over a period. Maximum drawdown is a key risk metric. Analysts use it to stress-test strategies and set risk limits — an important context for AI-generated risk alerts.
Tracking Error
The standard deviation of the difference between a portfolio's returns and its benchmark's returns. Low tracking error means the portfolio closely mirrors its index. An important metric when evaluating AI-driven portfolio construction tools.
Redemption Pressure
When investors withdraw capital from a fund, forcing the manager to sell holdings — sometimes at unfavourable prices. AI tools that monitor redemption signals give managers lead time to adjust before forced selling.
