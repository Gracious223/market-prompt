Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

This week's terms cover market structure and risk — the vocabulary the FCA, your portfolio managers, and your engineers all use when evaluating what AI is doing in real markets.

Algorithmic / Systematic Trading
Using rules-based or AI-driven systems to execute trades automatically. Regulators watch this closely because algos can amplify volatility — exactly what Rathi warned about at Mansion House.
Market Microstructure
The mechanics of how trades are actually executed: order books, bid-ask spreads, liquidity depth. AI agents operating at speed can distort microstructure in ways traditional regulation wasn't designed for.
Sharpe Ratio
A measure of risk-adjusted return — how much return a portfolio earns per unit of risk taken. If an AI tool claims to improve performance, your PMs will ask for the Sharpe, not just the return.
Drawdown
The peak-to-trough decline in a portfolio's value over a given period. Maximum drawdown is a key risk metric. PMs care as much about how much they could lose as how much they could gain.
AUM — Assets Under Management
The total market value of assets a fund or firm manages on behalf of clients. AUM is the primary scale metric in asset management — it determines fees, influence, and regulatory scrutiny.
Long-Only vs. Long/Short
Long-only funds only buy assets (traditional mutual funds, most ETFs). Long/short funds also "short" — bet on assets falling. Hedge funds typically run long/short strategies. Your users' strategies shape what AI signals are useful to them.

Lead Regulatory Story

BoE & FCA: 86% of UK Firms Use AI — Agents Widespread, But Real Autonomy Is Rare

The joint Bank of England and FCA fourth annual AI survey, published 26 October, reveals 86% of UK financial firms are now using AI (up from 75% in 2024), with generative AI making up 56% of deployed systems. About 80% of respondents run AI agents in production — but only 2% allow them to act with broad autonomy. Third-party concentration risk is rising: 53% of key AI systems rely on external providers.

Why It Matters

The 2% autonomy figure is a design constraint, not a ceiling. Build your product for the 98% who need human-in-the-loop — not the 2% edge case. Explainability and override controls aren't features, they're table stakes in this regulatory environment.

Research & Think About
  • Read the full BoE/FCA report at bankofengland.co.uk — it's free, and your competitors' governance leads will already have it
  • What does "third-party concentration risk" mean for your vendor strategy? Which of your AI dependencies are shared across the industry?
  • Map your product's AI decisions: which are decision-support, which are automated, and which have no human checkpoint?
