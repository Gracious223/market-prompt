Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Today's stories span valuations, execution infrastructure, and the mechanics of how markets move. These six terms — all new this edition — will sharpen your product discovery conversations with analysts and portfolio managers.

Short Selling
Borrowing shares to sell now, then buying back cheaper if the price falls. A directional bet on decline. Short sellers often surface fraud or overvaluation first — their research workflows are increasingly AI-assisted.
Market Maker
A firm continuously quoting buy (bid) and sell (ask) prices to provide liquidity. AI now updates quotes thousands of times per second. Your product's data latency matters to anyone sitting near this infrastructure.
Equity Risk Premium (ERP)
The extra return investors expect for holding equities over risk-free assets like gilts. When ERP narrows — as in an AI bubble — equities look expensive. AI tools monitor ERP in real time as a valuation signal.
Basis Points (bps)
The standard unit for interest rates and spreads: 100 bps = 1%. When analysts say "spreads tightened 25 bps," they mean 0.25%. You'll hear this constantly in fixed income and credit discussions.
Active Management
A portfolio manager making specific investment decisions to beat the market — selecting stocks, timing trades. The entire AI-for-analysts space exists to make active management more effective. Passive index funds don't need your product.
Liquidity Premium
The extra yield investors demand for holding less liquid assets — private credit, ABS, unlisted equity. AI tools that can value illiquid positions at scale are one of the highest-value emerging use cases in your space.

Singapore's IMDA released a Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI in January 2026 — the first regulatory body to address multi-agent autonomous deployment directly. Parliament Tells FCA: Publish AI Guidance by Year-End — or Face Scrutiny

The UK Treasury Committee's January 2026 report accused the FCA, Bank of England and HM Treasury of a "wait-and-see" approach to AI in financial services. Key demand: FCA must publish practical Consumer Duty and SM&CR guidance for AI outputs by end of 2026. HM Treasury's April response disputed the characterisation but did not reject the deadline.

Why It Matters
