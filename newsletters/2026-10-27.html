Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Today's stories touch on bond markets, IPO mechanics, and market rotation. Here are six terms distinct from previous editions that will help you hold your own in these conversations.

Credit Spread
The extra yield a corporate bond pays over a risk-free government bond (e.g. US Treasuries). Widening spreads signal investors demanding more compensation for risk — exactly what's happening in data centre bonds now.
IPO (Initial Public Offering)
First sale of a company's shares to the public. The S-1 is the registration filing. Anthropic's IPO will make its financials, risks, and enterprise contracts publicly visible for the first time — changing how you evaluate it as a vendor.
Junk Bond (Below Investment Grade)
Bonds rated below BBB- by S&P or Baa3 by Moody's. Higher yield, higher default risk. Nearly half of 2026 data centre issuance is junk-rated — significant for the AI infrastructure story your analysts are watching.
Beta
A stock's sensitivity to market movements. Beta > 1 means it moves more than the market; < 1 means less. High-beta AI stocks amplify gains in bull markets and losses in downturns — relevant context for AI theme exposure.
Duration
How sensitive a bond's price is to interest rate changes. Longer-duration bonds fall more when rates rise. Data centre bonds are often long-duration — making them doubly exposed when rates stay high and AI revenues disappoint.
Basis Points (bps)
One hundredth of one percentage point. 100 bps = 1%. Analysts use bps to describe small but meaningful changes in yields, spreads, or fees. "Spreads widened 150bps" means yields rose 1.5% above the benchmark.
