Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six more building blocks of market literacy — the vocabulary that earns credibility in the room with portfolio managers and analysts.

Short Selling
Borrowing shares to sell them today, with the obligation to buy them back later. Profitable if the price falls. AI tools increasingly scan filings and sentiment signals to identify short opportunities faster than any human can.
Basis Points (bps)
One hundredth of one percentage point (0.01%). Used to express precise changes in yields, spreads, or fees. "The fund's expense ratio fell 15bps" means it dropped from, say, 0.87% to 0.72%. Exact language signals financial fluency.
Maximum Drawdown
The largest peak-to-trough decline in portfolio value over a period. A core risk metric. If a fund's max drawdown was 30%, it fell 30% from its highest point before recovering. AI-managed funds like HIAI are measured against this constantly.
Market Capitalisation
Share price multiplied by shares outstanding. Large-cap (over $10B), mid-cap ($2–10B), small-cap (under $2B). Your analysts may focus on specific cap ranges — this shapes the data and benchmarks your product must handle.
Passive vs Active Management
Passive funds track an index (e.g. S&P 500) with minimal intervention. Active funds try to beat the index through stock selection. AI is blurring this line — HIAI is technically active, but its manager is a model, not a person.
Bid-Ask Spread
The gap between what a buyer will pay (bid) and what a seller will accept (ask). A narrower spread signals a more liquid market. High-frequency AI trading has structurally compressed spreads in liquid instruments — AI is a force here too.

New arXiv Paper Reframes Agentic Governance: "Capability Is Not Auditability"

Henry Han's "Governing Agentic AI in FinTech" (arXiv 2608.11344, Aug 2026) introduces the "Verifiability Gap" — the distance between what an agent does and what a firm can prove it did. Tested across nine model versions, frontier models produced no two identical executions: replay is impossible.

Why It Matters

Auditability is a product requirement, not just a legal one. If your agentic system cannot reconstruct its decisions, it cannot meet SM&CR accountability standards — regardless of how capable the underlying model is.

Research & Think About
  • Read arXiv 2608.11344 — specifically the "Verifiability Gap" framework and what it demands from product design
  • Under UK SM&CR: who owns accountability when an AI agent takes a regulated action on behalf of a firm?
  • How would you log, timestamp, and replay an agentic workflow for a regulator's audit request?
