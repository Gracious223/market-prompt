Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Your users speak a specific language. The faster you can engage with these concepts fluently, the more your product decisions will reflect real analyst and PM needs rather than assumptions about them.

Sharpe Ratio
Return earned per unit of risk taken. A Sharpe of 1.0 is acceptable; 2.0+ is strong. AI tools that improve signal-to-noise in research improve Sharpe by reducing noise-driven decisions.
Drawdown
The peak-to-trough decline in a portfolio's value. Maximum drawdown is a key risk metric. An AI product that surfaces downside scenarios earlier helps managers limit drawdown.
Tracking Error
How much a portfolio's returns deviate from its benchmark. Low tracking error = benchmark-hugging. High = active bets. AI tools that explain active bets help justify tracking error to clients.
Duration
A bond's sensitivity to interest rate changes. A 5-year duration means a 1% rate rise drops the bond's price ~5%. Fixed income AI tools must handle duration risk as a core input.
Credit Spread
The yield premium a corporate bond pays over a risk-free rate (e.g. Treasuries). Tight spreads signal confidence; widening spreads signal stress. Real-time spread monitoring is a common AI use case.
Rebalancing
Restoring a portfolio to its target allocation after market moves shift the weights. AI agents that can flag when rebalancing thresholds are breached — and model the tax implications — are high-value PM tools.
