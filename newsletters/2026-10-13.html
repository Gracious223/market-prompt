Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Hedge fund volatility, quant strategies, and concentrated positions were in focus this week. Here are six terms that will help you follow these conversations with confidence — and ask sharper questions of your users.

Short Selling
Borrowing shares to sell them now, expecting to buy them back cheaper later. It's a way to profit from falling prices — and a key reason crowded positions can unwind violently.
AUM (Assets Under Management)
The total market value of assets a firm manages on behalf of clients. It's the primary size metric in asset management — relevant when comparing the scale of AI deployment across firms.
Yield Curve
A graph of interest rates across bond maturities (3-month to 30-year). Its shape signals economic expectations — a "normal" upward slope vs. an inverted curve that historically precedes recession.
Beta
A measure of how much a stock moves relative to the overall market. Beta >1 means more volatile than the index; <1 means less. AI names have had elevated beta recently.
Market Cap
Total market value of a company's outstanding shares (share price × shares outstanding). It determines whether a stock is classified as large-cap, mid-cap, or small-cap in an index.
Basis Points (bps)
The standard unit for interest rates and yield changes. 1 basis point = 0.01%, so 100 bps = 1%. When the Fed raises rates by "25 bps," it means a 0.25% hike.

FCA Mills Review: Seven Recommendations and a 2030 Vision for Agentic Finance

The FCA published the Mills Review on 6 July 2026, setting seven concrete recommendations for AI-enabled financial services through 2030. Key asks: build trust and authentication frameworks specifically for agentic AI, use AI more actively in FCA supervision itself, and develop an AI-enabled public-interest financial capability service.

Why It Matters
