Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Today's sell-off, sector rotation, and talk of "valuation compression" surface terms your analysts use daily. Here are six concepts to sharpen your vocabulary — and your product intuition.

Volatility & the VIX
Volatility measures how much an asset's price moves over time. The VIX ("fear index") tracks expected S&P 500 volatility over 30 days. High VIX = market uncertainty. AI products that surface volatility signals in real time are highly valued.
Sector Rotation
When investors shift capital from one sector to another — e.g. from tech to financials — based on the economic cycle or changing valuations. This week's AI sell-off prompted exactly this. Your product must handle data across sectors, not just AI stocks.
Beta
A measure of how much a stock moves relative to the market. Beta of 1.5 means a stock typically moves 50% more than the index. High-beta AI stocks amplified this week's market downturn for portfolios holding them.
Valuation Compression
When a stock's price-to-earnings (P/E) multiple falls even if earnings stay flat — usually because investors decide they overpaid for future growth. Distinct from an earnings miss. This week's sell-off is primarily valuation compression, not an earnings story.
Earnings Per Share (EPS)
Net profit divided by shares outstanding. The single most watched metric in earnings season. AI tools increasingly help analysts model EPS sensitivity under different macro scenarios before results drop.
Rebalancing
Restoring a portfolio back to its target asset allocation after market moves shift the weights. After a sharp sector sell-off, PMs often rebalance systematically. AI tools that automate rebalancing triggers are among the most commercially successful applications.
