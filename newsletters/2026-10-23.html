Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Each edition rotates six terms your analysts and portfolio managers use daily. Build this vocabulary and you'll hold your own in any investment team conversation.

Short Selling
Borrowing shares and selling them, expecting to buy back cheaper. A source of market liquidity and price discovery — and a mechanism AI risk models are increasingly designed to monitor.
Earnings Per Share (EPS)
Net profit divided by shares outstanding. The most watched metric in equity analysis. AI tools that can extract and contextualise EPS surprises across earnings season are high-value at the buy side.
Duration
A bond's sensitivity to interest rate changes. A 10-year duration bond loses roughly 10% in value for every 1% rate rise. Fixed income analysts talk about duration the way equity analysts talk about beta.
Sector Rotation
Shifting capital between industry sectors as the economic cycle evolves. AI is increasingly used to detect early rotation signals in market microstructure data before they appear in price.
Prime Brokerage
A suite of services — lending, clearing, custody, margin — that banks provide to hedge funds and institutional investors. AI fraud and risk tools now sit inside prime brokerage infrastructure.
Sharpe Ratio
Risk-adjusted return: how much return per unit of volatility taken. A Sharpe above 1.0 is strong; above 2.0 is exceptional. AI-managed funds like HIAI will be judged against it from day one.

Bloomberg reports the Bank of England is discussing AI-caused market disruption as a potential theme for its next system-wide exploratory scenario (SWES), once the current private markets round concludes in 2027. Why It Matters

Research & Think About

  • Research "AI herding" in financial markets: what happens when multiple institutions use similar models trained on the same data?
  • Ask: if your product drove correlated decision-making across multiple clients, would that constitute a systemic risk? How would you design against it?
